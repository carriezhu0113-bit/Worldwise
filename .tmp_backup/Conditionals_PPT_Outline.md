# English Conditionals - 教学PPT大纲

## Slide 1: Title Slide
**Title:** English Conditionals (条件句)
**Subtitle:** Zero, First, Second, Third & Mixed Conditionals
**Level:** Intermediate to Advanced

---

## Slide 2: What are Conditionals?
- Conditional sentences = IF clause + Main clause
- IF clause = condition (条件)
- Main clause = result (结果)
- Two types:
  - **Real/Possible**: Zero & First Conditional
  - **Unreal/Hypothetical**: Second, Third & Mixed Conditionals

---

## Slide 3: Zero Conditional - 零条件句
**Structure:** If + Present Simple, Present Simple

**Use:** General truths, scientific facts, things that are ALWAYS true

**Examples:**
- If you heat water, it evaporates.
- If the wind blows, the trees move.
- If I drink coffee after 3 PM, I don't sleep at night.

**Key Point:** Same in the past, present, and future - never changes!

---

## Slide 4: Zero Conditional - More Examples
- If you leave ice cream in the sun, it melts.
- People get hungry if they don't eat.
- If you push this button, the lights come on.
- When you put a stone in water, it sinks.

**Note:** "If" and "When" have NO difference in meaning for general truths

---

## Slide 5: Zero Conditional - Imperatives
**Use with commands (祈使句):**
- If you accidentally drop some trash, pick it up.
- When you get home, call me.
- If Phil calls, don't answer the phone.

**Grammar:** If + Present Simple, Imperative (command)

---

## Slide 6: IF vs WHEN
| IF | WHEN |
|---|---|
| Maybe this will happen | I'm certain this will happen |
| "If you get home late..." | "When you get home..." |
| Uncertainty | Expectation |

**Example:**
- When you get home, call me. (I expect you to go home)
- If you get home late, call me. (Maybe you will, maybe you won't)

---

## Slide 7: First Conditional - 第一条件句
**Structure:** If + Present Simple, will + infinitive (base form)

**Use:** Possible future condition and its possible future result

**Examples:**
- If we don't leave soon, we'll miss our train.
- If I'm late for work again, I might lose my job.
- When you tell Dan the news, he's going to faint.

---

## Slide 8: First Conditional - Modal Verbs
**Not only "will" - other modals work too:**
- If we don't leave now, we **might** miss the train.
- If you study hard, you **could** pass the exam.
- If I see my boss, I **shall** ask her.
- If it gets colder, we **should** light a fire.

**Also:** can, could, may, might, going to

---

## Slide 9: First Conditional - Unless
**Unless = If...not**

**Examples:**
- Unless you get an invitation, you can't come to the party.
  = If you don't get an invitation, you can't come.
- Unless this program loads, I won't be able to finish my work.
  = If this program doesn't load, I won't be able to finish my work.

**Formula:** No invitation = No party / No load = No work

---

## Slide 10: Zero vs First Conditional - Comparison
| Zero Conditional | First Conditional |
|---|---|
| If I have time, I work out. | If I have time, I'll work out. |
| General truth | Specific future situation |
| Always true | Possible future |

**Zero:** I work out 2-3 times a week (habit)
**First:** Later today, if I have time, I'll work out (specific plan)

---

## Slide 11: Second Conditional - 第二条件句
**Structure:** If + Past Simple, would + infinitive

**Use:** Imaginary, unreal, hypothetical present or future situations

**Examples:**
- If I won the lottery, I would buy a mansion.
- If I were shorter, I would wear heels more.
- If I spoke French, I would be living in Paris.

**Important:** We use PAST tense but we're talking about PRESENT/FUTURE!

---

## Slide 12: Second Conditional - "Were" vs "Was"
**Use "WERE" with any subject (more formal/correct):**
- If I **were** you, I wouldn't worry.
- If he **were** younger, I would ask him out.
- If she **were** here, she would help us.

**"Was" is also possible but less formal**

**Common phrase:** "If I were you..." (giving advice)

---

## Slide 13: Second Conditional - Continuous Form
**Structure:** If + Past Simple, would be + V-ing

**Use:** Imaginary ongoing actions in the present

**Examples:**
- If I spoke French, I would be living in Paris.
- If I had an exam tomorrow, I would be revising right now.

---

## Slide 14: Third Conditional - 第三条件句
**Structure:** If + Past Perfect, would have + Past Participle

**Use:** Unreal/IMPOSSIBLE past situations (you cannot change the past!)

**Examples:**
- If I had studied harder, I would have passed my exam.
- If we had left earlier, we wouldn't have arrived late.
- If I hadn't tripped, I could have won the race.

**Reality:** I didn't study hard → I didn't pass

---

## Slide 15: Third Conditional - Form Review
**Past Perfect:** had + past participle (verb #3)
- eat → ate → eaten
- talk → talked → talked

**Spoken contractions:**
- I would have = I would've = I woulda (informal)
- If I had = If I'd

**Example:** If I'd studied harder, I would've passed my exam.

---

## Slide 16: All Four Conditionals - Comparison
| Conditional | IF Clause | Main Clause | Time | Reality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zero | Present Simple | Present Simple | Always | Real |
| First | Present Simple | will + verb | Future | Possible |
| Second | Past Simple | would + verb | Present/Future | Unreal |
| Third | Past Perfect | would have + PP | Past | Impossible |

---

## Slide 17: All Four - Same Situation, Different Meaning
**Situation: Getting there faster**

- **Zero:** If we drive, we get there faster. (general fact)
- **First:** If we drive, we'll get there faster. (specific future plan)
- **Second:** If we drove, we'd get there faster. (but we don't have a car)
- **Third:** If we had driven, we would have gotten there faster. (but we took the train and were late)

---

## Slide 18: Mixed Conditionals - Type 1
**Past condition → Present result**

**Structure:** If + Past Perfect, would + infinitive

**Examples:**
- If I had studied German in school, my German would be better.
  (Reality: I didn't study → My German isn't good now)
- If you hadn't broken up with me, we would still be together.
  (Reality: We broke up → We're not together now)
- If you had put your coat on, you wouldn't be sick.
  (Reality: You didn't put it on → You are sick now)

---

## Slide 19: Mixed Conditionals - Type 2
**Present condition → Past result**

**Structure:** If + Past Simple, would have + Past Participle

**Examples:**
- If I were rich, I would have bought that coat.
  (Reality: I'm not rich → I didn't buy it)
- If I lived closer to you, I would have come to your party.
  (Reality: I don't live close → I didn't come)

---

## Slide 20: Summary - Quick Reference
| Type | IF Clause | Main Clause | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zero | Present Simple | Present Simple | Always true |
| First | Present Simple | will/can/may/might + verb | Possible future |
| Second | Past Simple | would/could/might + verb | Unreal present/future |
| Third | Past Perfect | would/could have + PP | Unreal past |
| Mixed 1 | Past Perfect | would + verb | Past → Present |
| Mixed 2 | Past Simple | would have + PP | Present → Past |
