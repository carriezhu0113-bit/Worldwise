1️⃣名词与冠词

可数名词单复数
规则变化加-s/-es，不规则变化（child→children）
例：one book → two books
不可数名词
water, milk, information 等不能加-s
例：I need some water.
a / an 的用法
a + 辅音开头，an + 元音开头
例：a dog, an apple
the 的用法
特指、双方都知道的事物、独一无二的事物
例：The sun is bright.
名词所有格
's 表示"……的"
例：Tom's bag, my mother's car
some / any
some 用于肯定句，any 用于否定/疑问句
例：I have some apples. Do you have any?

2️⃣代词

人称代词主格与宾格
I/me, he/him, she/her, we/us, they/them
例：He likes me. I like him.
形容词性物主代词
my, your, his, her, its, our, their（后面必须带名词）
例：This is my book.
名词性物主代词
mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs（后面不带名词）
例：This book is mine.
反身代词
myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, themselves
例：I did it by myself.
this / that / these / those
近指/远指，单数/复数
例：This is my pen. Those are her books.

3️⃣动词时态

一般现在时
习惯、事实、经常性动作（注意第三人称单数加-s）
例：She goes to school every day.
一般过去时
过去发生的动作（规则动词加-ed，不规则动词需记忆）
例：I went to the park yesterday.
现在进行时
正在进行的动作：be + doing
例：They are playing football now.
一般将来时
will + 动词原形 / be going to + 动词原形
例：I will visit my grandma tomorrow.
现在完成时（基础）
have/has + 过去分词，表示过去的动作对现在的影响
例：I have already finished my homework.
情态动词 can / must / should
能力、必须、建议
例：I can swim. You must be quiet.

4️⃣介词

时间介词 in / on / at
in + 月/年/季节，on + 具体日期，at + 具体时刻
例：in May, on Monday, at 3 o'clock
地点介词 in / on / under / behind / between / next to / in front of
描述位置关系
例：The cat is under the table.
方向介词 to / from / into / out of / through
表示移动方向
例：Go to the door. Walk through the tunnel.
其他常用介词 with / without / about / for / of
搭配用法
例：a cup of tea, talk about, good at

5️⃣形容词与副词

形容词比较级
短词加-er，长词加 more，不规则：good→better, bad→worse
例：She is taller than me.
形容词最高级
短词加-est，长词加 most，前面加 the
例：He is the tallest in our class.
副词的基本用法
修饰动词，通常加-ly
例：She sings beautifully.
good / well 的区别
good 是形容词，well 是副词
例：She is a good student. She speaks English well.
much / many / a lot of / a few / a little
much + 不可数，many + 可数复数，a few + 可数，a little + 不可数
例：a few books, a little water

6️⃣句型结构

There is / There are
表示"有"，单数用 is，复数用 are
例：There is a cat. There are two dogs.
一般疑问句与否定句
be动词/助动词提前，not 构成否定
例：Do you like it? I don't like it.
特殊疑问句
what, where, when, who, why, how, which, how many/much/often/long
例：Where do you live? How old are you?
祈使句
动词原形开头，表示命令、请求、建议
例：Open the door. Don't run!
感叹句
What + (a/an) + adj. + n. / How + adj./adv.
例：What a beautiful day! How fast he runs!

7️⃣连词与从句

并列连词 and / but / or / so
连接并列成分
例：I like apples but I don't like bananas.
because 引导原因状语从句
表示原因
例：I'm happy because I passed the exam.
if / when 引导条件/时间状语从句
如果……/当……时候
例：If it rains, we will stay at home.
that 引导宾语从句（基础）
跟在 think, know, say 等动词后面
例：I think that he is right.
Zero Conditional（零条件句）
If + 一般现在时, 一般现在时（表示客观事实/普遍真理）
例：If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.
First Conditional（第一条件句）
If + 一般现在时, will + 动词原形（表示将来可能发生的情况）
例：If it rains tomorrow, I will take an umbrella.
who / which / that 引导定语从句（基础）
修饰前面的名词
例：The boy who is running is my brother.

8️⃣常用固定搭配

like / love / enjoy + doing
喜欢做某事
例：I enjoy reading books.
want / would like + to do
想要做某事
例：I want to go home.
be good at / be interested in / be afraid of
擅长/感兴趣/害怕
例：She is good at math.
look forward to + doing
期待做某事
例：I look forward to seeing you.
too + adj. / adj. + enough
太……/足够……
例：It's too cold. He is tall enough.
have to / don't have to
必须/不必
例：I have to finish my homework.
