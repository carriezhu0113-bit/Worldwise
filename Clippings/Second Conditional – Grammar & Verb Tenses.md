## Transcript

### Introduction

**0:01** · ESL Library Second Conditional What is the conditional mood in English?

**0:15** · We use the conditional mood for hypothetical situations.

**0:19** · When we don’t know if something will happen, we make a guess about the outcome.

**0:25** · What is the second conditional?

**0:27** · We use the second conditional for an unlikely or impossible outcome.

**0:32** · This means the outcome probably wouldn’t happen because the condition can’t or probably won't happen.

**0:40** · An example of an unlikely situation is: If I won the lottery, I would buy a tropical island.

**0:49** · Winning the lottery is a very unlikely condition, so the outcome of buying a tropical island would probably never happen.

**0:57** · An example of an impossible situation is: If I had wings, I would fly up to the stars.

**1:05** · Since people don’t have wings, this condition is impossible, so the outcome of flying to the stars wouldn’t happen.

### Sentence Patterns

**1:18** · The most common second conditional pattern is: if plus past, would plus verb.

**1:25** · Don’t be confused by the past verb in the “if” clause!

**1:31** · We use the second conditional for a present or future situation.

**1:36** · Note that we use a comma after the “if” clause.

**1:41** · For example: If I were the leader of this country, I would make many changes.

**1:50** · The “if” clause doesn’t have to start the sentence.

**1:54** · Another common pattern for the second conditional is: would plus verb, if plus past.

**2:03** · Note that we don’t use a comma before the “if” clause.

**2:07** · For example: I would make many changes if I were the leader of this country.

**2:15** · Did you notice that the past form of the Be verb is "were"?

**2:20** · "Was” becomes “were” in the “if” clause of a second conditional sentence no matter what subject pronoun or noun is used.

### Examples

**2:35** · Let’s look at some more examples of second conditional sentences in English.

**2:41** · If you became the CEO of the company, you would make a lot more money.

**2:50** · If our teacher just gave us the answers, we wouldn’t learn anything.

**2:58** · If I lived on the moon, I would have a great view of Earth.

**3:06** · They would have money to travel if they sold their house.

**3:14** · We would be very surprised if a celebrity came to our small hometown.

**3:22** · I’d stop eating junk food if my doctor told me to.
